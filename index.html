What people usually ask

Do I have to book ahead?

No. This is a walk-in shop first — show up, take a seat. Calling ahead just means less waiting if you'd rather.

How do I pay?

Tap, chip, swipe or cash — whatever's easiest for you.

Is the space accessible?

Yes — there's parking and a restroom on-site, and the salon is wheelchair accessible.

Will someone actually know what to do with curly hair?

Curl-pattern cutting and styling is one of the things this shop is known for — diffused, scrunched, or defined, it's up to you.

Where's the nail and wax counter?

Separate entrance, just around the corner on E 13th Street.

What hours are you open?

Every day, 9:30am to 7pm. Check the live status at the top of the page, or call to confirm.
